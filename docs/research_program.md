# research_program.v1

`research_program.v1` groups related hypotheses into durable research themes.

Programs organize work only. They do not authorize promotion, do not approve Lite usage, do not mutate runtime, and do not replace `research_hypothesis.v1` as the canonical research object.

Typical use:
- group related hypotheses
- record open questions
- summarize findings
- track regimes, instruments, and edge families under one theme

Statuses are `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `COMPLETE`, and `ARCHIVED`.
