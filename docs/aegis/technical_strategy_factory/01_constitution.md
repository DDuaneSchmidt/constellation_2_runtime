# AEGIS Technical Strategy Factory Constitution

## Purpose

This document defines permanent principles for the AEGIS Technical Strategy Factory. It does not define procedures.

## Mission

The factory exists to determine whether technical claims survive rigorous evidence review and can produce deployable knowledge. It does not exist to prove preconceived beliefs or defend famous trading systems.

## Principles

1. Evidence over opinion.
2. Claims are never proven; they are only supported, weakly supported, under review, falsified, or retired.
3. Refutation is more valuable than confirmation.
4. Historical popularity is not evidence.
5. Complexity requires justification.
6. Research must remain reproducible.
7. Supported claims remain under scrutiny.
8. Negative knowledge must be preserved.
9. The factory certifies evidence, not stories.
10. Observed effects and proposed explanations must remain separate.

## Anti-Drift Rule

If future changes conflict with this constitution, the constitution governs unless explicitly versioned and superseded.
