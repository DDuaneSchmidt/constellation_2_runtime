# HOSTILE_REVIEW_002

## Scope

Implementation reference: IMP_002 — 52-Week Breakout

Evidence source: BACKTEST_002

This hostile review attacks the evidence generated by BACKTEST_002. It does not certify IMP_002, does not certify CLAIM_001, and does not recommend capital allocation.

## Hostile Questions

| Question | Classification | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Is performance explained by lower exposure? | CONCERN | IMP_002 was in market 45.91% of the full period. It improved drawdown versus buy-and-hold and beat random timing, but it did not beat the 50/50 baseline on total return or CAGR. Lower exposure remains a major explanatory candidate. |
| Is performance concentrated in one period? | CONCERN | The clearest benefits appear in 2000-2003, 2008, and 2022 downside-avoidance periods. The 2020 subperiod was weak, and validation-period return capture lagged both registered baselines. |
| Does validation survive? | CONCERN | Validation-period CAGR was positive at 6.00% and max drawdown was lower than buy-and-hold. However, validation Sharpe and CAGR lagged both buy-and-hold and the 50/50 baseline, and drawdown was not better than the 50/50 baseline. |
| Does the edge survive higher costs? | PASS | At 20 bps round-trip, CAGR was 3.71% and Sharpe was 0.507. At 50 bps round-trip, CAGR was 3.30% and Sharpe was 0.457. Higher costs degrade but do not erase the evidence. |
| Does the edge survive execution delay? | PASS | The 1-day execution-delay stress reported CAGR of 3.80%, Sharpe of 0.511, and max drawdown of -19.89%, broadly similar to the base case. |
| Do breakout perturbations behave similarly? | PASS | The 200-day breakout perturbation had CAGR of 4.41% and Sharpe of 0.578. The 300-day breakout perturbation had CAGR of 3.56% and Sharpe of 0.497. These are directionally similar to the 252-day base case. |
| Does IMP_002 beat random timing? | PASS | IMP_002 ranked at 100% on random CAGR and Sharpe. Raw random drawdowns were much worse than IMP_002, although the max-drawdown percentile field needs documented direction convention. |
| Are drawdown improvements meaningful? | CONCERN | Full-period max drawdown improved from -55.20% for buy-and-hold to -17.89% for IMP_002 and from -32.30% for 50/50 to -17.89%. In validation, drawdown was similar to the 50/50 baseline rather than better. |
| Are return reductions acceptable? | CONCERN | Full-period CAGR declined from 8.35% for buy-and-hold and 4.40% for 50/50 to 3.84% for IMP_002. Validation-period CAGR lagged both baselines. Acceptability depends on the intended use case and cannot be decided by this factory. |
| Is the implementation deployable? | CONCERN | The rules are simple, auditable, and low-frequency enough for practical execution. Deployment remains constrained by return give-up, tax context, operational controls, and the need for paper validation. This factory does not allocate capital. |

## Stress Review Summary

| Review area | Result |
|---|---|
| Higher costs | Survives 20 bps and 50 bps stress with lower but positive metrics. |
| Execution delay | Survives 1-day delay in this run. |
| Breakout perturbation | 200-day and 300-day breakout reviews are directionally similar. |
| Exit perturbation | 50DMA exit is materially weaker; 150DMA exit is materially stronger. Exit rule sensitivity remains a concern. |
| Random timing | Strong on CAGR and Sharpe; max-drawdown percentile direction should be documented. |
| Baselines | Beats buy-and-hold on drawdown and slightly on full-period Sharpe; does not beat buy-and-hold on return. Does not beat 50/50 on full-period or validation CAGR. |

## Hostile Findings

1. IMP_002 provides evidence of a simple breakout implementation that is not explained by randomized timing alone.
2. IMP_002 materially reduces full-period drawdown versus buy-and-hold.
3. IMP_002 does not dominate the 50/50 exposure baseline and gives up substantial return versus buy-and-hold.
4. Validation-period evidence is not strong because CAGR and Sharpe lag both registered baselines.
5. Exit-rule perturbation behavior is uneven and should be addressed before any stronger implementation decision.
6. Evidence is one SPY-only implementation and cannot certify CLAIM_001.

## Impact

Claim impact: WEAK_POSITIVE

Implementation impact: mixed

The evidence is weak positive for CLAIM_001 only because a second reasonable implementation produced non-random, stress-resistant evidence. It is not sufficient to certify CLAIM_001.
