# HOSTILE_REVIEW_003

## Scope

Implementation reference: IMP_003 — Turtle Breakout

Evidence source: BACKTEST_003

This hostile review attacks the evidence generated by BACKTEST_003. It does not certify IMP_003, does not certify CLAIM_001, and does not recommend capital allocation.

## Hostile Questions

| Question | Classification | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Is performance explained by lower exposure? | CONCERN | IMP_003 was in market 48.01% of the full period. It did not beat the 50/50 SPY/cash baseline on CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, or ulcer index. Lower exposure explains a meaningful part of the drawdown reduction. |
| Is performance concentrated in one period? | CONCERN | The strongest subperiod evidence is 2008 downside avoidance. Other reviewed periods show weaker behavior, including negative results in 2000-2003 and 2022 and underperformance versus both baselines in 2020. |
| Does validation survive? | FAIL | Validation-period return remained positive, but IMP_003 lagged both buy-and-hold and 50/50 SPY/cash on CAGR, Sharpe, Sortino, and ulcer index, and had worse drawdown than the 50/50 baseline. |
| Does the edge survive higher costs? | CONCERN | Higher costs do not erase positive returns, but 50 bps round-trip cost reduces CAGR to 1.48%, Sharpe to 0.215, and max drawdown to -31.39%. |
| Does the edge survive execution delay? | CONCERN | The 1-day execution-delay stress remains positive but weakens CAGR, Sharpe, and max drawdown versus the base case. |
| Do parameter perturbations behave similarly? | CONCERN | Entry and exit perturbations remain directionally positive, but results vary materially. The 30-day low exit worsens max drawdown to -34.72%, while 40-day and 70-day breakout variants are stronger than the base case. |
| Does IMP_003 beat random timing? | PASS | IMP_003 ranked at 99.40% on random CAGR and 100.00% on random Sharpe. Raw random drawdowns were much worse than IMP_003, although the max-drawdown percentile field needs documented direction convention. |
| Are drawdown improvements meaningful? | CONCERN | Full-period drawdown improved versus buy-and-hold and slightly versus the 50/50 baseline. Validation-period drawdown was worse than the 50/50 baseline, and the full-period ulcer index was worse than the 50/50 baseline. |
| Are return reductions acceptable? | FAIL | Full-period CAGR declined from 8.35% for buy-and-hold and 4.40% for 50/50 SPY/cash to 2.71% for IMP_003. Validation-period CAGR lagged both baselines materially. |
| Is the implementation deployable? | CONCERN | The rules are simple and auditable, but trade count is high relative to prior implementations, return capture is weak, validation is unfavorable, and tax and operational burdens remain unresolved. This factory does not allocate capital. |
