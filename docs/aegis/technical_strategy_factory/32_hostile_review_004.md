# HOSTILE_REVIEW_004

## Scope

Implementation reference: IMP_004 — Dual Momentum

Evidence source: BACKTEST_004

This hostile review attacks the evidence generated by BACKTEST_004. It does not certify IMP_004, does not certify CLAIM_001, and does not recommend capital allocation.

## Hostile Questions

| Question | Classification | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Is performance explained by lower exposure? | CONCERN | IMP_004 was in market 74.20% of the full period. It beat buy-and-hold and the 50/50 baseline on full-period CAGR and Sharpe, so lower exposure does not fully explain the result. Exposure reduction remains a contributor to drawdown improvement. |
| Is performance concentrated in one period? | CONCERN | Development-period evidence is strong, especially 2000-2003 and 2008. Validation remains positive but weaker versus buy-and-hold and the 50/50 baseline on several risk-adjusted measures. |
| Does validation survive? | CONCERN | Validation-period CAGR was 11.20%, above the 50/50 baseline but below buy-and-hold. Validation Sharpe lagged both baselines and drawdown was worse than the 50/50 baseline. |
| Does the edge survive higher costs? | PASS | At 20 bps round-trip, CAGR was 8.42% and Sharpe was 0.668. At 50 bps round-trip, CAGR was 8.31% and Sharpe was 0.660. Higher costs degrade modestly but do not erase the evidence. |
| Does the edge survive execution delay? | PASS | The 1-day execution-delay stress reported CAGR of 8.32%, Sharpe of 0.661, and max drawdown of -33.70%, broadly similar to the base case. |
| Do perturbation/stress variants behave similarly? | PASS | The 9-month, 15-month, weekly, and quarterly perturbations remain directionally positive. 15-month is stronger than base case, while 9-month, weekly, and quarterly variants are weaker but still positive. |
| Does IMP_004 beat random timing? | PASS | IMP_004 ranked at 100% on random CAGR and Sharpe. Raw random drawdowns were much worse than IMP_004, although the max-drawdown percentile field needs documented direction convention. |
| Are drawdown improvements meaningful? | CONCERN | Full-period max drawdown improved from -55.20% for buy-and-hold to -33.70% for IMP_004. The 50/50 baseline had slightly better full-period drawdown and materially better validation-period drawdown. |
| Are return reductions acceptable? | PASS | Full-period CAGR exceeded buy-and-hold and the 50/50 baseline. Validation-period CAGR lagged buy-and-hold but remained above the 50/50 baseline. |
| Is the implementation deployable? | CONCERN | The rules are simple, monthly, auditable, and low turnover. Deployment review still requires paper validation, tax review, operational controls, and acceptance of validation-period baseline tradeoffs. This factory does not allocate capital. |
