# Discovery Candidate Promotion Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines the requirements for promoting Discovery Candidates through the active review states of the AEGIS Investment Thesis Factory.

Promotion is a research governance decision. It is not an investment recommendation, trade idea, portfolio decision, valuation opinion, or allocation instruction. A promoted candidate remains subject to future contradiction, score degradation, retirement, or rejection.

## 2. Promotion Philosophy

Promotion exists to preserve only candidates that have survived hostile review.

Promotion must be difficult.

Promotion must be evidence-first.

Promotion must preserve the reasoning available at the time of decision.

Every candidate is assumed invalid until supporting evidence exceeds contradiction.

## 3. Promotion Scope

This standard applies to promotion between the following statuses:

- CANDIDATE.
- UNDER_REVIEW.
- SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE.
- PROMOTED_CANDIDATE.

Each promotion step must be documented separately. A candidate may not skip required review evidence because later reviewers believe the conclusion is obvious.

## 4. Non-Goals

Promotion does not authorize:

- Security selection.
- Trade advice.
- Portfolio action.
- Capital allocation.
- Manual trade capture.
- Automated execution.
- Candidate population at scale.

Promotion only means the candidate is eligible for the next research stage defined by Discovery Candidate governance.

## 5. Universal Promotion Requirements

Every promotion requires:

- Four-filter pass.
- Evidence exceeds contradiction.
- Documented value capture path.
- Documented consequence chain.
- Sufficient Discovery Quality Index.
- Completed review protocol.

Failure in any requirement blocks promotion.

## 6. Four-Filter Pass Requirement

Promotion requires documented pass status for:

- Truth.
- Importance.
- Underappreciation.
- Investability.

Each filter must include supporting evidence, contradictions, alternative explanations, and reviewer rationale. A strong result in one filter cannot compensate for failure in another filter.

## 7. Evidence Exceeds Contradiction Requirement

Promotion requires a direct comparison between supporting evidence and contradictory evidence.

The comparison must document:

- Supporting evidence strength.
- Contradiction strength.
- Alternative explanations.
- Evidence gaps.
- Residual uncertainty.

Lack of contradictory evidence is not sufficient for promotion. The candidate must have affirmative evidence that survives review.

## 8. Value Capture Path Requirement

Promotion requires a documented value capture path.

The value capture path must answer:

- Who could capture value?
- Why would value accrue there?
- What could prevent value capture?
- What evidence supports the path?

Value capture plausibility is not a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, or trade any security.

## 9. Consequence Chain Requirement

Promotion requires a documented consequence chain from observed reality to potential economic relevance.

The chain must identify:

- Present reality.
- First-order consequences.
- Second-order consequences.
- Economic impact.
- Potential investment relevance.
- Weak links.
- Assumptions.

The consequence chain must not rely on narrative coherence, hindsight, or unstated assumptions.

## 10. DQI Requirement

Promotion requires sufficient Discovery Quality Index.

DQI must reflect:

- Evidence quality.
- Four-filter strength.
- Consequence depth.
- Value capture plausibility.
- Contradiction penalty.
- Falsifiability.

DQI must not be inflated to justify a preferred outcome. If DQI is insufficient or unsupported, promotion is blocked.

## 11. Completed Review Protocol Requirement

Promotion requires completion of the Discovery Candidate Review Protocol.

The completed review must include:

- Evidence review.
- Contradiction review.
- Alternative explanation review.
- Four-filter reassessment.
- Consequence chain review.
- Value capture review.
- Scoring reassessment.
- Status recommendation.

Incomplete review blocks promotion regardless of analyst conviction.

## 12. CANDIDATE to UNDER_REVIEW

A candidate may advance from CANDIDATE to UNDER_REVIEW only when:

- The candidate claim is defined clearly.
- Initial evidence exists.
- The candidate is falsifiable.
- The candidate can undergo contradiction review.
- Required registry fields are present.

This transition means the candidate deserves review. It does not mean the candidate is supported.

## 13. UNDER_REVIEW to SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE

A candidate may advance from UNDER_REVIEW to SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE only when:

- All four filters pass initial hostile review.
- Evidence quality is sufficient for independent review.
- Contradictions are documented.
- Alternative explanations are documented.
- Consequence chain plausibility is established.
- Scores and DQI are justified.

This transition means the candidate has support. It does not mean the candidate is ready for thesis development.

## 14. SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE to PROMOTED_CANDIDATE

A candidate may advance from SUPPORTED_CANDIDATE to PROMOTED_CANDIDATE only when:

- Supporting evidence exceeds contradiction.
- Consequence chain is economically meaningful.
- Value capture path is documented.
- DQI is sufficient under current governance.
- Review protocol is complete.
- Promotion rationale is preserved.
- Promotion audit trail is complete.

This transition makes the candidate eligible for further thesis development. It does not authorize investment action.

## 15. Prohibited Promotion Bases

Promotion must never occur because of:

- Narrative quality.
- Research effort invested.
- Analyst enthusiasm.
- Lack of contradictory evidence.
- Seniority of reviewer.
- Timeliness of theme.
- Desire to keep the research pipeline full.

Any promotion rationale depending on these factors is invalid.

## 16. Promotion Rationale Preservation

Every promotion decision must preserve:

- Promotion date.
- Prior status.
- New status.
- Reviewer identity or role.
- Evidence basis.
- Contradiction assessment.
- Alternative explanations considered.
- Scores and DQI at the time of decision.
- Promotion rationale.
- Remaining weaknesses.

Later evidence must not be used to rewrite the original promotion basis.

## 17. Promotion Audit Trail

Every promotion requires an audit trail that allows later reviewers to reconstruct:

- What was promoted.
- When it was promoted.
- Who reviewed it.
- What evidence supported it.
- What contradicted it.
- Why contradiction did not block promotion.
- What score changes occurred.
- What future review triggers were set.

Promotion without an audit trail is invalid and must be reversed or reopened.

## 18. Governance Rules

- No promotion without hostile review.
- No promotion without contradiction review.
- No promotion without documented value capture path.
- No promotion without documented consequence chain.
- No promotion without sufficient DQI.
- No promotion by narrative appeal.
- No promotion by absence of disproof.

Promotion governance must preserve the distinction between discovery research and investment evaluation.

## 19. Outputs

This standard supports production of:

- Promotion Decision Record.
- Promotion Audit Trail.
- Updated Scorecard.
- Updated Status History.
- Promotion Rationale Record.

Outputs must be reviewable, durable, and suitable for later contradiction analysis.
