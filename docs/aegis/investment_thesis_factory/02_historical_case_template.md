# Historical Case Validation Template

## 1. Purpose

Use this template to evaluate historical investment outcomes without hindsight bias.

The objective is not to explain why a company became successful. The objective is to determine whether an underappreciated reality existed before the outcome became obvious, whether that reality was observable using only information available at the time, and whether it could have produced a falsifiable Discovery Candidate.

Assume the factory is wrong until evidence suggests otherwise.
