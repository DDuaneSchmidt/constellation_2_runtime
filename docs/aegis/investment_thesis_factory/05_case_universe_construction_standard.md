# Case Universe Construction Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines the methodology for constructing the historical case universe used throughout the Discovery Validation Program.

The objective is to create a representative, unbiased, and research-grade set of historical investment outcomes suitable for testing CLAIM_001 and future factory claims. The case universe must be treated as flawed until its construction process is documented, challenged, and validated.

## 2. Why Case Universe Construction Matters

The case universe determines what the validation program can and cannot prove. A biased universe can make weak archetypes appear predictive, convert familiar stories into false evidence, and hide failure modes that would invalidate the factory.

Case construction must therefore be evaluated as part of the evidence, not as a neutral input.

## 3. Core Risks

- Survivorship Bias: selecting only companies or industries that survived long enough to become visible.
- Selection Bias: choosing cases because they fit a preferred thesis or are easier to analyze.
- Narrative Bias: preferring cases with clean stories over cases with messy or contradictory evidence.
- Recency Bias: overweighting recent outcomes because documentation is easier to obtain or memories are fresher.
- Availability Bias: selecting familiar companies, geographies, or sectors because they are easier to recall.
- Hindsight Bias: allowing known outcomes to influence discovery dates, classification, or interpretation.

## 4. Universe Design Principles

The case universe must be:

- Representative.
- Repeatable.
- Auditable.
- Cross-industry.
- Cross-decade.
- Cross-geography.
- Balanced.

These principles are requirements, not aspirations. Any exception must be documented as a bias exposure.

## 5. Eligible Case Definition

Eligible cases must involve:

- Exceptional long-duration investment outcomes.
- Clearly identifiable discovery periods.
- Sufficient historical evidence.
- Observable information available at the time.

Eligibility does not imply that a case supports CLAIM_001. It only means the case is suitable for unbiased evaluation.

## 6. Ineligible Case Definition

Cases are ineligible when they have:

- Insufficient historical information.
- Purely speculative outcomes.
- Outcomes dependent on unavailable historical data.
- Discovery dates that cannot reasonably be established.

Cases must also be excluded when the available record is too thin to separate pre-outcome evidence from retrospective narrative.

## 7. Historical Coverage Requirements

The universe must target coverage across:

- Multiple decades.
- Multiple market regimes.
- Multiple economic environments.

No single historical period may dominate the universe unless the imbalance is explicitly justified and recorded.

## 8. Industry Coverage Requirements

The universe must include representation from:

- Consumer.
- Industrial.
- Healthcare.
- Financial.
- Technology.
- Infrastructure.
- Energy.
- Business Services.

Industry representation should be broad enough to test whether archetypes generalize beyond favored sectors.

## 9. Geography Coverage Requirements

The universe must include multiple regions and must avoid U.S.-only bias.

Geographic coverage should account for differences in disclosure quality, market structure, capital access, regulation, and investor base. Cases from less familiar regions must not be excluded solely because they require more documentation effort.

## 10. Outcome Coverage Requirements

The universe should contain:

- Exceptional Winners.
- Moderate Winners.
- Failures.
- False Positives.
- Near Misses.

Outcome diversity is mandatory because CLAIM_001 cannot be tested only against successful cases.

## 11. Winner Selection Rules

Winner selection must be based on:

- Investment outcome quality.
- Not fame.
- Not popularity.
- Not narrative appeal.

Selection rationale must specify the objective outcome criteria used and the evidence supporting inclusion.

## 12. Failure Selection Rules

The universe must include:

- Businesses that appeared attractive but failed.
- Businesses matching archetype characteristics but lacking exceptional outcomes.

Failure cases must be selected with the same discipline as winner cases. They are not secondary evidence.

## 13. False Positive Selection Rules

The universe must include cases that would likely have passed an initial thesis review but later failed.

False positives must be used to test whether the factory can distinguish real discovery candidates from superficially convincing cases.

## 14. Near-Miss Selection Rules

The universe must include cases where the observed reality was largely correct but value capture or recognition failed.

Near misses are required to test whether correct insight is sufficient, whether consequence chains fail, and whether investability can be evaluated separately from truth.

## 15. Sampling Methodology

Sampling must require:

- Predefined selection criteria.
- Documented inclusion rationale.
- Documented exclusion rationale.

Selection rules must be established before case scoring. Any supplemental case additions must identify whether they are filling a coverage gap, testing a contradiction, or correcting a documented bias exposure.

## 16. Anti-Survivorship Controls

The universe must include mandatory minimum representation of:

- Failures.
- False positives.
- Near misses.

Exact percentages must not be set in this standard. They require future governance determination and must be documented in the approved case universe charter.

## 17. Anti-Hindsight Controls

The following rules are mandatory:

- Discovery dates must be established before analysis.
- Only information available at the discovery date may be used.
- Outcome information cannot influence inclusion decisions.

Cases must be rejected or quarantined when the research team cannot separate pre-outcome evidence from post-outcome explanation.

## 18. Universe Quality Metrics

Each approved universe must be scored from 1-10 on:

- Industry Diversity.
- Geographic Diversity.
- Outcome Diversity.
- Historical Coverage.
- Evidence Quality.
- Bias Resistance.

Scores must include written justification. A high score in one category cannot offset an unexamined weakness in another.

## 19. Governance Rules

- Case additions must be documented.
- Case removals must be documented.
- Selection rationale must be preserved.
- Universe changes must be auditable.

No case may be added solely because it supports a preferred archetype, claim, or factory conclusion.

## 20. Universe Maintenance Rules

The universe must be periodically reviewed for:

- Coverage.
- Bias exposure.
- Representation balance.
- Evidence quality.

Maintenance reviews must identify whether changes improve evidence quality or merely make the universe more convenient.

## 21. Outputs

The case universe construction process must produce:

- Approved Case Universe.
- Universe Quality Scorecard.
- Coverage Report.
- Bias Assessment Report.

Outputs must preserve enough detail for an independent reviewer to reconstruct selection logic.
