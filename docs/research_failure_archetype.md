# research_failure_archetype.v1

`research_failure_archetype.v1` preserves recurring failure patterns found during offline research or Lite feedback review.

Failure archetypes are advisory research memory. They may inform future governance proposals, but they do not automatically become governance rules and cannot promote anything to Aegis Lite.

Use them for:
- recurring invalidation conditions
- observable warning signs
- repeated stop/risk failure patterns
- examples linked to evidence packets and conclusions

Any governance or Lite change still requires a separate `research_to_lite_promotion.v1` review and human approval.
