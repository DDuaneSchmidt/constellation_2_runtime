# Atlas V2 External Strategy Claim Principles

External strategy claims are externally sourced descriptions to classify, deduplicate, and cheap-test. They are not strategies to trade.

V1 accepts only explicit operator-supplied text: pasted transcripts, pasted titles and URLs, and manual notes. It does not scrape YouTube, crawl channels, download media, or call a captions API.

Every extracted object preserves the source boundary. No claim is treated as true, profitable, validated, advisable, or ready for execution.

Atlas V2 consumers must query the recorded claim, mechanism, dedupe, and handoff records. Consumers may not invent truth from titles, channels, code, or external reputation.
