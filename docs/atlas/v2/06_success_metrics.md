# Atlas V2 Success Metrics

Initial success metric:

Atlas can create a complete chain:

AttentionDecision -> Prediction -> Outcome -> Regret -> CalibrationRecord -> BehaviorChange -> ExperienceEvent

The initial validation suite must prove:

- Every required field is present.
- Every ExperienceEvent links back to a Decision, Prediction, and Outcome.
- Every BehaviorChange has a trigger and reason.
- No discovery, generation, trading, sleeve, candidate, paper-position, or capital-allocation artifacts are created.

Future metrics must stay tied to outcome-linked experience, calibration, regret, behavior change, and future decision improvement.
