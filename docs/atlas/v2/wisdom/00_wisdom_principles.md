# Atlas V2 Wisdom Principles

Atlas wisdom is reusable behavior-changing learning. It is not discovery, hypothesis generation, opportunity generation, ingestion, or a knowledge graph.

Core principles:

- Reality outranks Atlas. Wisdom must remain subordinate to observed outcomes and verified experience.
- Learning outranks activity. New action volume is not evidence of wisdom.
- Behavior change is the evidence of learning. A statement that does not change future behavior is an observation.
- Wisdom exists only if it changes future behavior. CandidateWisdom must be validated through WisdomValidation and WisdomEvent records.
- No wisdom is exempt from challenge. CONTRADICTED or decaying evidence moves wisdom to CONTESTED or RETIRED.
- Adaptive wisdom outranks static wisdom. Strong historical support does not protect wisdom from new outcomes.

Non-goals:

- No hypothesis generators.
- No discovery engines.
- No YouTube ingestion.
- No knowledge graphs.
- No new opportunity systems.

Canonical chain:

ExperienceEvent -> CandidateWisdom -> WisdomValidation -> WisdomEvent -> improved future decision

A CandidateWisdom statement must link to originating ExperienceEvent records and supporting Outcome records. Without both links it is not admissible wisdom.
