# Forbidden Boundaries

No high-volume claim may automatically become:

- candidate
- sleeve
- paper position
- recommendation
- validation
- trade

The pipeline must not add:

- trading authority
- broker execution
- autonomous execution
- sleeves
- candidates
- paper positions
- capital allocation
- recommendations
- validation authority
- allocation surfaces

The pipeline must not create synthetic hypotheses. Contrarian theory statements are deterministic fragility summaries derived from supplied claim text and classified mechanisms. They are not promoted hypotheses and carry no execution, validation, recommendation, or allocation authority.
