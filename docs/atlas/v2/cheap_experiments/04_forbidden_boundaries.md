# Forbidden Boundaries

Cheap experiments are record/read/audit only.

They must not create or authorize:

- discovery systems
- hypothesis generators
- YouTube ingestion
- candidates
- sleeves
- paper positions
- trades
- broker execution
- autonomous execution
- capital allocation
- recommendations
- validation authority

Cheap experiments may produce learning evidence and ExperienceEvent records. That evidence can later change Atlas behavior only through the core loop and append-only transition history.
