# Historical Experience Coverage Principles

Atlas V2 Historical Experience Coverage Expansion V1 improves recognition of existing Atlas and AEGIS artifacts. It does not create new research, claims, candidates, sleeves, paper positions, validation authority, recommendations, trading authority, broker execution, autonomous execution, or allocation surfaces.

Coverage expansion is extraction only. A parser may preserve an expectation, outcome, contradiction, decision, lesson learned, confidence, or failure reason only when the source artifact contains that information. Missing expectation remains missing. Missing outcome remains missing.

All converted records remain append-only Atlas V2 learning evidence. Consumers must query verified truth and ledger records; they must not infer readiness or authority from parser output.
