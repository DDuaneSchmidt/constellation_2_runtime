# Source Specific Rules

ADR documents map explicit `Decision` text to a historical decision and explicit `Consequences` text to observed outcome when present. `Context` may supply expectation. An ADR without consequences remains incomplete for outcome.

Investment Thesis Factory documents map claim, null hypothesis, success criteria, or purpose sections to expectation. Failure criteria, counterexample rules, contradiction controls, or decision framework sections may supply outcome or contradiction evidence when present.

Technical Strategy Factory documents map claim, scope, purpose, success criteria, or test objective sections to expectation. Result review status, final verdict, metrics, impact, hostile findings, known limitations, or required next evidence sections may supply outcome or contradiction evidence when present.

Research journal reports map expected outcome, claim, decision, or prior belief sections to expectation. Actual outcome, result, conclusion, review result, strongest contradictory evidence, root cause, lesson, or behavior-change sections may supply outcome or lesson evidence.

Failure reports map explicit expected fields to expectation and explicit failed, actual, root cause, or failure-reason fields to outcome/failure reason. They must not collapse unknown expectation into failed outcome.
