# Forbidden Boundaries

Generated claims are research claims, not trading instructions. They must include uncertainty and a contrarian prompt, and they must describe intended testability.

Circular label-integrity evidence is blocked unless the generated claim is specifically about label failure.

Forbidden outputs remain: hypotheses, cheap experiment specs, experiment execution, experiment results, candidates, sleeves, paper positions, trades, orders, broker execution, autonomous execution, capital allocation, allocation surfaces, recommendations, validation authority, and live-market authority.
