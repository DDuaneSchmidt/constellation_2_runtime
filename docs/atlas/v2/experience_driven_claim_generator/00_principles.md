# Experience-Driven Claim Generator Principles

Atlas V2 Experience-Driven Claim Generator V1 originates bounded research claims from existing Atlas evidence only.

Allowed inputs are `ExperienceEvent`, `Regret`, `CalibrationRecord`, `HistoricalExperienceRecord`, `MechanismRegistryEntry`, `ExternalStrategyContrarianTheory`, label-integrity evidence about label failure, and learning-estimator reports.

The generator emits `GeneratedResearchClaim` and `ClaimGenerationRun` only. It does not create hypotheses, cheap experiment specs, experiment results, executions, candidates, sleeves, paper positions, allocation surfaces, recommendations, validation authority, broker authority, autonomous execution, or live-market access.
