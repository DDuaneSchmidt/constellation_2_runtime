# Boundaries

Allowed inputs are existing Atlas artifacts: `HistoricalExperienceRecord`, `ExperienceEvent`, `MechanismRegistryEntry`, `ExternalStrategyClaim`, `ExternalStrategyContrarianTheory`, `LabelIntegrityReport`, and LearningEstimator report records.

The generator does not use internet access, does not crawl YouTube, does not fetch URLs, and does not infer market truth. Circular or failed label-integrity inputs are rejected as normal mechanism claims unless the generated record is explicitly about label failure.
