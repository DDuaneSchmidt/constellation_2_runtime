# Atlas V2 Claim Idea Generator V1

The Claim Idea Generator originates bounded research claims from existing Atlas V2 artifacts only. It writes `GeneratedResearchClaim`, `ClaimGenerationRun`, and `ClaimGenerationSourceSummary` records.

It does not create hypotheses, experiment specs, experiment results, ExperienceEvents, candidates, sleeves, paper positions, allocations, recommendations, validation decisions, broker actions, live-market access, or trades. Generated claims are framed as uncertain research claims and must include mechanism family, uncertainty, contrarian prompt, and intended testability.
