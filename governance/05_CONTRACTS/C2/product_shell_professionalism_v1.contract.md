# product_shell_professionalism_v1

Behavioral requirements:
- the home shell MUST render governed product summary artifacts instead of stitching top-of-screen priorities locally
- blocked and actionable states MUST remain visually distinct
- authority labels and drill-down refs MUST remain visible on current product surfaces
- where governed truth now exists, placeholder or internal-tool seam language MUST be removed or reduced

Anti-pattern bans:
- shell-local ranking
- page-local blocked suppression
- page-local changed-since-review comparison
- AI-local actionability framing
