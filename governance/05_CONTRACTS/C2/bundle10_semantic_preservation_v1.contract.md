# bundle10_semantic_preservation_v1

This contract ratifies the semantic preservation boundary for Bundle 10.

Bundle 10 is:
- a tax runtime materialization bundle
- a governed tax decision-support bundle
- a tax-aware advisory binding bundle

Bundle 10 is not:
- a control-plane rewrite
- an advisory semantics rewrite outside the governed tax-binding seam
- a trust-plane rewrite
- a strategy expansion
- a generic tax platform expansion

Bundle 10 MUST preserve:
- Bundle 9 bounded-obligation pipeline semantics
- Bundle 8 advisory decision semantics outside the governed tax-binding seam
- Bundle 7 release/readiness semantics
- Bundle 6 trust-plane semantics
- Bundle 5 transition semantics
- Bundle 4 certification and boundary semantics
- Bundle 3 configuration activation semantics
