# refinement_product_safety_preservation_v1

Refinement may not optimize away trust-preserving distinctions.

Top-level or secondary visibility must be preserved when required for:
- blocked-but-important states
- critical degraded states
- claim-strength distinctions
- `insufficient_evidence`
- `not_yet_observable`
- release/readiness blockers
- any upstream trust-preserving distinction already governed by Bundles 3-13

Banned refinement behaviors:
- suppressing a blocked state behind a convenience summary
- collapsing claim-strength differences into a more confident summary
- hiding degraded states that materially change actionability
- removing drill-down access for preserved governed proof
