# policy_trust_preserving_override_v1

Policy evolution may not weaken or suppress upstream trust-preserving protections for:
- blocked-but-important visibility
- critical degraded visibility
- claim-strength visibility
- `insufficient_evidence`
- `not_yet_observable`
- release/readiness blockers
- preserved drill-down access guaranteed by Bundle 14 refinement rules

Override laws:
- trust-preserving overrides block reduce-emphasis proposals
- trust-preserving overrides block compression proposals when compression would blur or hide the protected distinction
- when a prior policy conflicts with a trust-preserving override, rollback or preserve MUST dominate
