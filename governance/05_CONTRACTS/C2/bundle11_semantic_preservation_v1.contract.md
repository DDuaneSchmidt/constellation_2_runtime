# bundle11_semantic_preservation_v1

Bundle 11 is:
- a proactive opportunity and review bundle
- a bounded scenario-significance binding bundle
- a thin operator/advisory usefulness bundle

Bundle 11 is not:
- a control-plane rewrite
- a trust-plane rewrite
- an advisory semantics rewrite outside the governed opportunity-binding seam
- a tax semantics rewrite
- a strategy expansion
- a generic task-management system

Bundle 11 MUST preserve:
- Bundle 10 tax-state semantics
- Bundle 9 bounded-obligation pipeline semantics
- Bundle 8 advisory decision semantics outside the governed opportunity-binding seam
- Bundle 7 release/readiness semantics
- Bundle 6 trust-plane semantics
- Bundle 5 transition semantics
- Bundle 4 certification and boundary semantics
- Bundle 3 configuration activation semantics
