## 1. Executive Summary

Atlas V1 should prioritize capabilities that make existing evidence easier to find, reuse, synthesize, and challenge. Tonight's evidence does not support building autonomous research authority, new search-space machinery, capital allocation logic, or validation authority.

The highest-ROI build-now capabilities are:

- Research Journal: continue structured capture of observations, knowledge, failures, and reviews.
- Negative Knowledge: preserve failed assumptions and corrected misdiagnoses so weak ideas are not repeatedly investigated.
- Research Librarian: retrieve existing evidence and source context before new analysis begins.
- Research Synthesis: compress scattered AEGIS artifacts into support, contradiction, limitation, and next-evidence summaries.

The next useful tier is bounded and assistant-only:

- Hypothesis Generation AI: only after constrained by journal evidence, negative knowledge, and falsification requirements.
- Experiment Design AI: to improve controls, disconfirmation plans, and evidence requirements before research begins.
- Lightweight Research Contrarian AI: to convert skepticism into testable evidence checks, not vetoes or authority.

Atlas should defer graph expansion, search-space creation/evolution, research economics, and research history automation. Atlas should prohibit Chief Scientist authority, autonomous validation authority, and autonomous capital allocation while evidence remains underpowered and runtime truth forbids consumers from inventing truth.

## 2. Evidence From Tonight

Journal Review 002 identified outcome maturity as the strongest bottleneck. Paper observations and open positions accumulated faster than statistically useful, distributed closed outcomes. It also showed that architecture readiness, paper workflow progress, and candidate volume are repeatedly misread as evidence maturity unless prior knowledge and failures are reused.

Root Cause Review 001 found that AEGIS-generated paper trades have not become compelling research candidates because the system is still producing early observations rather than mature evidence. On the reviewed day, 63 paper positions produced 12 included closed or resolved validation samples, 51 excluded samples, 0 validated hypotheses, and 0 capital-review-ready rows.

Outcome Maturity Acceleration Review 001 concluded that the fastest useful learning path is deterministic follow-through on existing open paper positions. It did not recommend new architecture, new candidates, sleeve changes, capital allocation, or runtime truth changes.

Evidence Accumulation Forecast Review 001 found that evidence accumulation is uneven and concentrated. The clearest near-term forecast was only 0 to 2 additional validation samples unless existing deterministic closure thresholds are reached. Capital-review relevance was not forecastable from sample count alone.

C2 Trend EQ Outcome Review 001 showed that `C2_TREND_EQ_PRIMARY_V1` is the only meaningful closed-sample evidence stream, but not a proven superior strategy. Its evidence is measurable, underpowered, concentrated, and early-realized performance is cautionary.

Open Paper Position Outcome Follow-Through Review 001 found 51 open positions across high-priority outcome-learning sleeves, all with source exit recommendation `HOLD`. No reviewed open position was already eligible for deterministic closure. The highest learning value remained evidence follow-through, not intervention.

Atlas Roadmap Reconciliation Review 001 concluded that the long-term Atlas direction is useful, but near-term value is evidence compression, retrieval, synthesis, and negative-knowledge reuse. Search-space expansion, economics, historian behavior, validation authority, and Chief Scientist authority were deferred or prohibited.

Knowledge entries reinforce the same pattern: architecture readiness can exceed evidence maturity, closed outcomes are the binding constraint, paper workflow is not live readiness, candidate generation needs certification and governance evidence, research triage is more informative than capital review, and verified-graph blockers can come from evidence-ID contract mismatches.

Failure entries preserve the strongest warnings: architecture maturity did not imply evidence maturity, candidate volume did not proxy for quality, capital allocation review was premature, standalone technical claims failed broad support, and low sleeve production did not prove poor sleeve quality.

## 3. Top AEGIS Bottlenecks

1. Outcome maturity. Closed, usable, distributed outcomes remain the primary constraint. The strongest evidence stream has 12 usable samples and remains underpowered.

2. Evidence maturity versus architecture maturity. Verified graph readiness and auditability do not prove empirical readiness or runtime authority.

3. Candidate conversion interpretation. Raw signals and rejection counts mix expected governance suppression, certification bottlenecks, and true candidate-quality questions.

4. Evidence synthesis and retrieval. Tonight's reviews depended on repeatedly finding and reconciling journal entries, outcome artifacts, sleeve diagnostics, graph evidence, and failure records.

5. Repeated research waste. The journal repeatedly corrected the same misdiagnoses: paper progress as readiness, candidate volume as quality, architecture as evidence, and attention triage as capital readiness.

6. Evidence contract and source freshness. Existing valid artifacts can still fail graph visibility or interpretation if IDs, authorities, or source freshness are not aligned.

7. Premature autonomy risk. Validation authority, capital allocation, and strategy control would create false confidence while hypotheses remain underpowered or blocked.

## 5. Capabilities To Build Now

Research Journal: BUILD_NOW.

Reason: Journal Review 002 and `KNW_0013` show the journal improved understanding by compressing repeated evidence gaps into reusable claims. It addresses evidence maturity, repeated waste, and synthesis without adding autonomy.

Recommended next evidence check: verify whether future reviews continue reusing existing OBS, KNW, FAIL, and report evidence rather than creating duplicate findings.

Negative Knowledge: BUILD_NOW.

Reason: `FAIL_0004` through `FAIL_0016` captured recurring failed assumptions around architecture maturity, paper readiness, candidate volume, technical claims, capital review, and sleeve quality. Negative knowledge directly reduces repeated research waste.

Recommended next evidence check: before each new research review, require a short negative-knowledge scan for prior failures relevant to the question.

Research Synthesis: BUILD_NOW.

Reason: Tonight's reviews produced value by reconciling outcome maturity, root cause, candidate conversion, and Atlas roadmap evidence into ranked bottlenecks. This capability improves synthesis of existing evidence without requiring years of history.

Recommended next evidence check: compare future synthesis outputs against source reports for unsupported conclusions, missing contradictions, and overclaims.

Research Librarian: BUILD_NOW.

Reason: The work depended on retrieving multiple journal reports, knowledge entries, failure entries, and AEGIS diagnostics. A bounded librarian capability would reduce duplicate work and improve source coverage.

Recommended next evidence check: measure whether retrieval finds all required source reports and relevant KNW/FAIL entries before a review begins.

## 6. Capabilities To Build Next

Hypothesis Generation AI: BUILD_NEXT.

Reason: It can become useful after evidence retrieval, synthesis, and negative knowledge are stable. It should generate only falsifiable, evidence-grounded hypotheses and must not create candidates, trade advice, sleeve changes, or validation conclusions.

Recommended next evidence check: test whether generated hypotheses cite existing evidence, state null hypotheses, include disconfirmation criteria, and avoid known failed assumptions.

Experiment Design AI: BUILD_NEXT.

Reason: Technical Strategy Factory failures show that claims need better controls, relationship-specific evidence, and falsification plans before promotion. Experiment design can improve evidence maturity if it remains advisory.

Recommended next evidence check: review whether proposed validation plans include contemporaneous evidence requirements, contradictory evidence, alternative explanations, and clear failure criteria.

Lightweight Research Contrarian AI: BUILD_NEXT.

Reason: The journal repeatedly corrected overconfident interpretations. A bounded contrarian can help by asking what would falsify a claim and identifying missing evidence, without authority to veto or expand scope indefinitely.

Recommended next evidence check: verify that contrarian outputs become specific evidence checks rather than narrative objections.

## 7. Capabilities To Defer

Knowledge Graph AI: DEFER.

Reason: Broad graph expansion depends on more mature outcome history. Current value is retrieval and synthesis over existing journal objects and verified artifacts, not a new graph layer.

Recommended next evidence check: defer until the journal has enough repeated, stable relationships that human-readable retrieval becomes insufficient.

Search Space Evolution AI: DEFER.

Reason: AEGIS lacks enough mature, distributed outcome evidence to know which search spaces should expand, shrink, merge, or retire.

Recommended next evidence check: revisit only after multiple sleeves have sufficient closed outcomes and validated or rejected discovery paths.

Search Space Creator AI: DEFER.

Reason: The bottleneck is not lack of search spaces; it is outcome maturity, candidate conversion, and evidence accumulation. Creating more spaces would increase underpowered surface area.

Recommended next evidence check: require proof that existing paper-active streams have been followed through and still leave a discovery coverage gap.

Research Economist AI: DEFER.

Reason: Research economics and capital-adjacent prioritization are premature with 0 capital-review-ready rows. It may later help estimate research effort versus evidence value, but not allocation.

Recommended next evidence check: revisit only when research quality artifacts show validated or capital-review-ready rows.

Research Historian AI: DEFER.

Reason: The research history is too short for historian automation. The Research Journal is the correct near-term history mechanism.

Recommended next evidence check: revisit after enough journal cycles exist to reveal recurring long-horizon patterns that simple reports cannot summarize.

## 8. Capabilities Prohibited For Now

Validation AI with autonomous authority: PROHIBIT_FOR_NOW.

Reason: Validation authority belongs to deterministic evidence and runtime truth. AI must not certify truth, mark hypotheses validated, override validation gates, or convert underpowered evidence into conclusions.

Recommended next evidence check: if a non-authoritative validation reviewer is proposed later, verify it only flags missing evidence and never changes validation state.

Chief Scientist AI: PROHIBIT_FOR_NOW.

Reason: Chief Scientist authority would centralize strategy before the evidence base is mature. Current value is synthesis and bounded critique, not autonomous research direction.

Recommended next evidence check: do not revisit authority until Atlas has a long record of accurate, audited, non-authoritative synthesis.

Autonomous capital allocation: PROHIBIT_FOR_NOW.

Reason: Tonight's evidence shows 0 capital-review-ready hypotheses or sleeves. Autonomous allocation would create premature autonomy and false confidence from underpowered evidence.

Recommended next evidence check: prohibition remains until deterministic research quality and runtime truth artifacts independently support capital-review readiness.

Autonomous search-space control: PROHIBIT_FOR_NOW.

Reason: Search space creation, retirement, mutation, or evolution by AI would expand architecture and authority before outcome evidence matures.

Recommended next evidence check: none for V1; keep search-space authority outside Atlas V1.

## 9. Final Recommendation

Atlas V1 should be a research-memory and evidence-synthesis layer, not an autonomous research authority.

Top 3 Atlas V1 investments:

1. Research Librarian: retrieve existing journal and diagnostic evidence before new analysis.
2. Research Synthesis: produce evidence-grounded support, contradiction, limitation, and next-check summaries.
3. Negative Knowledge: surface relevant failures and corrected misdiagnoses before researchers repeat weak paths.

Continue the Research Journal as the underlying memory surface. Build Hypothesis Generation AI, Experiment Design AI, and lightweight Research Contrarian AI next only as bounded assistants after retrieval and synthesis discipline is stable.

Defer Knowledge Graph expansion, Search Space Evolution, Search Space Creator, Research Economist, and Research Historian capabilities. Prohibit Chief Scientist authority, autonomous validation authority, autonomous capital allocation, and autonomous search-space control for Atlas V1.

Recommended next step: create a bounded Atlas V1 Research Librarian specification that retrieves and cites existing Research Journal reports, knowledge entries, failure entries, and AEGIS diagnostic artifacts without creating new architecture or authority.
