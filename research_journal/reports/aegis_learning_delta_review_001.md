Objective: compare AEGIS understanding at the start of tonight versus the end of tonight.

## Initial Beliefs

At the start of tonight, AEGIS had a useful but still broad understanding of its research state:

- The Research Journal appeared useful for understanding, but its strongest value was still being tested across individual observations.
- Architecture readiness, verified graph readiness, paper workflow progress, candidate volume, and research allocation artifacts were still easy to overread as signs of broader maturity.
- Outcome maturity was suspected to be a binding constraint because paper observations were accumulating faster than closed, usable validation outcomes.
- Low or absent candidate production still needed explanation: it could have reflected sleeve weakness, normal no-signal behavior, data gaps, governance suppression, certification defects, or conversion bottlenecks.
- Macro calendar readiness still looked like a possible future research path, but the class of blocker was not fully separated between data dependency, implementation gap, governance suppression, and premature search space.
- Capital review and research attention allocation needed separation because research artifacts could produce allocation-like language while evidence remained underpowered.
- Safety gates looked restrictive, and the live question was whether they protected research quality or materially slowed learning.
- Atlas was expected to help retrieval and synthesis, but it was not yet clear whether it changed AEGIS conclusions or only made review faster.

The initial state was therefore useful but under-specified: AEGIS knew it had evidence gaps, but many gaps were still broad categories rather than ranked bottlenecks.

## Revised Beliefs

By the end of tonight, AEGIS has a narrower and more operationally useful understanding:

- Architecture readiness is not evidence maturity. Verified, auditable surfaces can coexist with underpowered outcomes, blocked runtime authority, and immature research evidence.
- Paper workflow progress is research observability, not live readiness. Paper positions and review-only candidates can advance while trade advice, broker execution, live trading, and real capital remain disabled.
- Research attention allocation is currently more useful than capital review. Existing artifacts can rank attention, pause, redesign, hold, decrease, or investigate, but all reviewed capital-readiness surfaces remain at 0 eligible rows.
- Candidate volume is not a quality proxy. Raw signals and candidate counts must be split into expected portfolio suppression, certification bottlenecks, contract validity, paper-path availability, and outcome maturity.
- Low sleeve candidate production is not currently proven to be sleeve weakness. Existing diagnostics support a mixed interpretation: healthy no-signal behavior, threshold conditions, governance suppression, data needs, certification bottlenecks, and underpowered evidence must be cleared before labeling a sleeve weak.
- Macro calendar readiness is a mixed class with primary data dependency. It is a legitimate future research opportunity, but current evidence is `NEEDS_SOURCE` / `NEEDS_DATA`, with no paper path, no candidates, no observations, and no validation samples.
- Safety gates are appropriately restrictive. They delayed actionability and authority, not read-only learning. They prevented fabricated events, forced validation, premature promotion, unsupported capital review, trade advice, broker execution, and real capital claims.
- Outcome maturity is the primary decision bottleneck, but not the deepest causal bottleneck. The deeper bottleneck is measurable, distributed evidence flow through candidate, certification, paper observation, deterministic outcome, validation sample, and evidence maturity.
- Atlas did not change any diagnosis. It improved retrieval, reuse trails, and compression, but human review remained necessary for ranking, source reconciliation, fragility assessment, and scope control.

## Corrected Misdiagnoses

The main corrected diagnoses were:

1. Architecture maturity implied evidence maturity.
   Revised: architecture and verified graph readiness can be auditable while evidence remains underpowered or runtime authority remains blocked.

2. Paper workflow progress implied live readiness.
   Revised: paper workflow is a research surface only. It does not imply trade advice, broker execution, live trading, manual capture, or real capital authority.

3. Candidate volume or low production directly measured candidate or sleeve quality.
   Revised: candidate interpretation requires stage separation. Raw volume, expected portfolio suppression, certification defects, contract validity, paper observations, and closed samples are different evidence surfaces.

4. Outcome maturity was the root cause by itself.
   Revised: outcome maturity is the primary downstream decision bottleneck, but measurable-flow generation is the deeper causal bottleneck.

5. Macro workflow expansion could repair macro readiness.
   Revised: macro readiness is data-bound. Missing macro events should not be repaired by fabricated events, forced shadow validation, autonomous sleeve creation, or expanded authority.

6. Safety gates might be accidental blockers to learning.
   Revised: safety gates preserved learning while blocking unsupported actionability.

7. Atlas might materially change research conclusions.
   Revised: Atlas changed process efficiency and evidence retrieval, not the conclusions themselves.

## Stable Conclusions

Several diagnoses survived challenge and became more robust:

- Closed, usable, distributed outcomes remain the binding constraint for capital-readiness claims.
- No reviewed artifact supports capital review or real capital allocation.
- Research attention triage has value now, provided it remains read-only and clearly separated from trade sizing or allocation instructions.
- Candidate volume alone remains a poor quality proxy.
- Broad standalone technical-indicator claims remain weak; technical evidence must be relationship-specific, baseline-aware, and out-of-fixture tested.
- Runtime truth dependencies outrank architecture preference, including legacy compatibility decisions.
- Daily warnings are useful caution signals and should not be treated as clean success or ignored noise until recurrence or clearance is checked.
- The journal itself is useful because it compresses recurring evidence gaps into reusable claims without adding trading authority or new architecture.

## New Bottleneck Hierarchy

The bottleneck hierarchy changed from broad evidence immaturity to a ranked causal chain:

1. Measurable, distributed evidence flow.
   This is now the deepest supported bottleneck. AEGIS does not yet convert enough hypotheses into valid, certified, governed, distributed paper observations that can later become closed validation samples.

2. Paper-observation to outcome latency.
   Existing open positions are the immediate downstream blockage. The outcome decomposition found 51 high-priority open positions, all with `HOLD` exit recommendations, and 47 with no near-term deterministic closure proximity.

3. Outcome maturity and validation sufficiency.
   This remains the primary decision bottleneck: 0 validated hypotheses, 0 capital-review-ready rows, underpowered hypotheses, and concentrated included samples.

4. Evidence concentration.
   The strongest usable samples remain concentrated in `HYP_LARGE_CAP_EQUITY_MOMENTUM_20_60D_V1` / `C2_TREND_EQ_PRIMARY_V1`, limiting generalization even when sample count improves.

5. Candidate conversion, certification, and paper-path availability.
   The 42 raw signals to 1 generated candidate pattern is now understood as a mixed funnel, not one failure mode. Certification and paper-path issues are high-leverage because they determine whether future outcomes can exist outside the current concentration.

6. Data readiness for specific paths.
   Macro calendar remains the clearest data dependency. It matters, but it is path-specific rather than the universal explanation for current outcome scarcity.

7. Sleeve inactivity or no-signal behavior.
   This became less important as a primary diagnosis. It remains a monitoring target, but current evidence does not support broad sleeve weakness.

8. Raw search-space expansion.
   This became the least supported leverage point. More search surface, more architecture, or more raw candidate volume does not solve evidence maturity without measurable flow and validation.

Bottlenecks that became more important:
- Candidate-to-paper measurability.
- Paper observation to deterministic outcome follow-through.
- Evidence concentration across sleeves and hypotheses.
- Certification/actionability and evidence-contract continuity.
- Negative-knowledge reuse before repeating prior failed assumptions.

Bottlenecks that became less important:
- Broad architecture expansion.
- Capital review.
- Raw candidate volume.
- Broad weak-sleeve diagnosis.
- Macro workflow expansion without a governed data source.
- Atlas as an autonomous research judge.

## Architecture Impact

Several architecture ideas were deprioritized:

- New journal architecture: the frozen, human-readable journal already improved understanding and should not be expanded before reuse value is observed.
- Runtime truth or governance changes: the evidence points to data, certification, measurable-flow, and outcome-maturity gaps, not a need to modify truth logic or safety gates.
- Sleeve or candidate-generation changes: current evidence does not justify changing sleeves or generation rules before no-signal, threshold, governance, certification, contract, and paper-path diagnostics are separated.
- Macro workflow expansion: macro readiness needs a governed source, not event fabrication, forced validation, or autonomous paper sleeve behavior.
- Capital allocation architecture: capital review remains premature while all readiness surfaces show 0 eligible rows.
- Atlas autonomy: Atlas should remain read-only retrieval and synthesis support. Its most useful missing capability is negative knowledge / failure reuse, not authoritative decision-making.
- Legacy cleanup by preference: deprecation should wait until runtime truth no longer requires the compatibility layer.

Architecture impact is therefore mostly negative in the useful sense: tonight reduced pressure to build new mechanisms and increased pressure to use existing evidence surfaces more precisely.

## Final Assessment

Classification: Major Learning.

Reason: AEGIS did not merely add detail to existing beliefs. It changed the causal diagnosis. The strongest shift was from broad evidence immaturity and possible architecture/search expansion toward a sharper bottleneck model: measurable, distributed evidence flow is the root bottleneck; outcome maturity is the downstream decision bottleneck; capital review, raw volume, broad architecture, and safety-gate relaxation are not supported next steps.

The end-of-night understanding is more conservative and more useful. It preserves research learning, avoids unsupported actionability, and gives future reviews concrete trigger conditions: watch for non-Trend included closed samples, capital-review emissions, HEALTHY_NO_CANDIDATE sleeves failing under favorable conditions, resolved paper/data blockers that still produce no measurable flow, and any runtime truth or policy change touching capital or trading authority.
