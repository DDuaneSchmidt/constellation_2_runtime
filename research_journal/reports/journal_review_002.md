# Research Journal Review 002

Objective: synthesize learning produced by the Research Journal so far and identify the highest-leverage AEGIS bottlenecks.

Scope: OBS_0001 through OBS_0020, KNW_0004 through KNW_0019, FAIL_0004 through FAIL_0016, Journal Review 001, and weekly_learning_report.md.

This review creates no new observations, knowledge objects, failures, architecture, mechanisms, DES systems, DES-Lite systems, or runtime truth changes.

## 1. Top 10 Learnings

1. Architecture readiness is not evidence maturity. KNW_0004 and FAIL_0004 show that auditable architecture and verified graph readiness can coexist with underpowered outcomes, blocked runtime authority, and immature research evidence.

2. Closed outcomes are the binding constraint for many research claims. KNW_0006, KNW_0017, FAIL_0006, and OBS_0002 show that paper observations and open positions accumulated faster than statistically useful, distributed closed outcomes.

3. Research triage is more useful than capital review while evidence is underpowered. KNW_0011, OBS_0006, OBS_0012, OBS_0013, and FAIL_0010 show differentiated attention decisions but 0 capital-review-ready hypotheses or sleeves in the reviewed evidence.

4. Paper workflow progress is not live readiness. KNW_0005, OBS_0011, FAIL_0005, and safety-gate evidence show paper tracking can progress while trade advice, broker execution, live trading, and real capital remain disabled.

5. Candidate generation needs certification and governance context before it becomes useful evidence. KNW_0007, KNW_0015, OBS_0007, OBS_0016, OBS_0017, FAIL_0007, and FAIL_0014 show raw-signal and candidate counts mix expected portfolio suppression, certification bottlenecks, and quality questions.

6. Low sleeve candidate production did not prove poor sleeve quality. KNW_0014 and FAIL_0014 show non-producing sleeves can reflect valid no-signal conditions, data needs, and candidate-generation gates rather than primary sleeve-quality failure.

7. Technical Strategy Factory evidence must be relationship-specific and split by evidence type. KNW_0008, KNW_0018, FAIL_0008, and FAIL_0016 show broad standalone indicator claims remained unsupported, false-positive-prone, baseline-recoverable, or non-generalizing, while context-linked structures need separate validation.

8. Runtime truth dependencies outrank architecture preference. KNW_0010, OBS_0018, and FAIL_0011 show legacy compatibility layers should not be retired while runtime truth still consumes them.

9. Warnings are useful learning inputs, not clean success. KNW_0012, OBS_0019, FAIL_0013, and FAIL_0015 show daily integrity WARN states can preserve non-blocking but recurring caution signals.

10. The journal improved understanding by compressing evidence gaps into reusable claims. KNW_0013, OBS_0020, and Journal Review 001 show the journal helped name repeated gaps without adding trading authority or new architecture.

## 2. Top 5 AEGIS Bottlenecks

1. Outcome maturity. Evidence strength: strongest. OBS_0002, OBS_0006, OBS_0012, KNW_0006, KNW_0011, KNW_0017, FAIL_0006, and FAIL_0010 repeatedly show few or concentrated closed outcomes, many open positions, 0 validated hypotheses, and 0 capital-review-ready rows. This blocks stronger research claims and capital review.

2. Evidence maturity versus architecture maturity. Evidence strength: strong. OBS_0005, OBS_0009, OBS_0018, KNW_0004, KNW_0010, FAIL_0004, FAIL_0011, and FAIL_0013 show readiness surfaces can be auditable while runtime authority, empirical confidence, or legacy-retirement readiness remains blocked.

3. Candidate conversion, certification, and governance interpretation. Evidence strength: strong. OBS_0001, OBS_0007, OBS_0016, OBS_0017, KNW_0007, KNW_0014, KNW_0015, FAIL_0007, FAIL_0009, and FAIL_0014 show raw signals must be separated into expected suppression, certification defects, data needs, and actual candidate-quality evidence.

4. Discovery quality and Technical Strategy Factory generalization. Evidence strength: strong for standalone-claim rejection, partial for context-linked promise. OBS_0003, KNW_0008, KNW_0018, FAIL_0008, and FAIL_0016 show broad technical discovery claims did not survive evidence review, even where controlled or context-linked tests improved.

5. Evidence contract and certification resolution. Evidence strength: moderate but concrete. KNW_0019 shows verified-graph blockers can arise from evidence-ID contract mismatches even when artifacts exist and validate. OBS_0016 also shows certification status can block otherwise scored raw signals. This bottleneck is narrower than outcome maturity but high leverage because it can make existing evidence undiscoverable or non-actionable.

## 3. Top 5 Misdiagnoses Corrected

1. Original belief: architecture maturity implies evidence maturity.
Evidence review: OBS_0005, OBS_0009, Journal Review 001, KNW_0004, and FAIL_0004 showed auditable graph or architecture surfaces did not imply mature outcomes or runtime authority.
Revised understanding: architecture readiness and evidence maturity must be judged separately.

2. Original belief: paper workflow progress indicates live readiness progress.
Evidence review: OBS_0011, KNW_0005, and FAIL_0005 showed paper tracking progressed while safety gates kept live trading, broker execution, trade advice, and real capital disabled.
Revised understanding: paper progress is a research workflow signal, not live-readiness evidence.

3. Original belief: candidate volume or low candidate production directly indicates candidate or sleeve quality.
Evidence review: OBS_0001, OBS_0007, OBS_0016, OBS_0017, KNW_0007, KNW_0014, KNW_0015, FAIL_0007, FAIL_0009, and FAIL_0014 showed candidate counts mixed valid no-signal conditions, expected portfolio suppression, certification blockers, and data needs.
Revised understanding: candidate quality must be judged after separating suppression, certification, and governance effects.

4. Original belief: standalone technical-indicator evidence supports durable Technical Strategy Factory claims.
Evidence review: OBS_0003, KNW_0008, KNW_0018, FAIL_0008, and FAIL_0016 showed broad technical claims were unsupported, false-positive-prone, baseline-recoverable, or non-generalizing.
Revised understanding: technical evidence must be relationship-specific and split by mechanism, context, regime, and out-of-fixture support.

5. Original belief: capital allocation review becomes useful once research artifacts exist.
Evidence review: OBS_0006, OBS_0012, OBS_0013, KNW_0011, and FAIL_0010 showed research artifacts produced attention triage, but 0 hypotheses or sleeves were ready for capital review.
Revised understanding: current value is research attention allocation, not capital allocation.

## 4. Areas Proven Not To Be Primary Bottlenecks

- Poor sleeve quality as the primary explanation for low candidate production. FAIL_0014 and KNW_0014 show the reviewed evidence supported normal no-signal conditions, data needs, and candidate-generation gates instead.

- Safety gates as an accidental blocker to learning. OBS_0010 and KNW_0009 show read-only governance preserved learning while preventing premature action.

- Lack of journal architecture. OBS_0008, OBS_0020, KNW_0013, and Journal Review 001 show the small journal already improved understanding; no evidence supported adding architecture before reuse value is observed.

- Macro workflow expansion as a repair for missing macro readiness. OBS_0015 and FAIL_0012 show missing or immature data should not be repaired by fabricating events, expanding authority, or creating autonomous sleeve behavior.

- Legacy cleanup by architecture decision alone. OBS_0018, KNW_0010, and FAIL_0011 show runtime truth dependencies, not preference, decide deprecation readiness.

## 5. Areas Still Unresolved

- Whether candidate quality improves after certification and portfolio-suppression effects are separated from raw volume.

- Whether outcome maturity becomes distributed across sleeves and hypotheses rather than concentrated in one sleeve or hypothesis.

- Whether daily research integrity warnings clear, recur, or escalate after stale candidate inputs and sleeve-health follow-through items are addressed.

- Whether context-linked Technical Strategy Factory evidence can generalize out of fixture.

- Whether evidence-ID contract mismatches are isolated incidents or a recurring discovery-quality bottleneck.

- Whether underpowered hypotheses move toward validation readiness with additional closed outcomes, or remain structurally blocked.

## 6. Recommended Next Investigation

Evidence-check OBS_0007: Candidate generation volume alone did not imply candidate quality.

Justification: this is the highest-leverage next investigation because multiple strong learnings converge on the same funnel: OBS_0001 found low candidate production was mixed-cause, OBS_0016 split raw-signal rejection into expected portfolio suppression and certification bottlenecks, OBS_0017 showed governance suppression before actionability, KNW_0007 and KNW_0015 warn against using volume as quality, and FAIL_0007 records that candidate volume was not a reliable quality proxy. Outcome maturity remains the strongest overall bottleneck, but candidate-quality interpretation is the next unresolved diagnostic needed to prevent future reviews from mistaking throughput counts for learning progress.
