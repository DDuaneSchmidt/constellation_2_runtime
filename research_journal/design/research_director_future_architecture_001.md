# Research Director Future Architecture 001

## Scope

This is design-only future architecture documentation. It does not implement production code, runtime authority, trading authority, capital authority, or candidate promotion.

## Core Architecture Position

Scientist-as-primary-pillar is denied.

Scientist-as-supporting-adversarial-service is approved.

Research Director / Meta-Research / Research Allocation are higher long-term priorities.

## Future Architecture

The Research Director should decide what the research system studies next, based on evidence quality, learning value, opportunity cost, repeated failure patterns, mechanism diversity, and paper-forward capacity constraints.

The Research Adversary / Assumption Extractor / Constraint Analyzer should support the Research Director by producing critique artifacts. It should not become the director.

## Research Director Responsibilities

- Allocate research attention.
- Compare mechanism families.
- Track evidence maturity.
- Prioritize falsification.
- Identify stale or redundant research.
- Balance exploration and exploitation.
- Decide which research questions deserve more observation.

## Adversarial Service Responsibilities

- Extract assumptions.
- Produce competing explanations.
- Produce null explanations.
- Identify constraints.
- Propose experiments.
- Propose falsification tests.
- Suggest belief updates for human/system review.

## Allowed Outputs

- AnomalyNarrative
- MechanismProposal
- CompetingExplanation
- NullExplanation
- AssumptionExtraction
- ConstraintAnalysis
- ExperimentProposal
- FalsificationProposal
- BeliefUpdateProposal

## Forbidden Outputs

- TradeRecommendation
- CapitalRecommendation
- PositionSizing
- PortfolioAllocation
- CandidatePromotion
- ReplayOverride
- QualificationOverride
- GovernanceOverride

## Required Artifact Questions

- Why might this be true?
- Why might this be false?
- What assumptions are required?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence weakens it?
- What would falsify it?
- What experiment should test it?
- What authority does this artifact NOT have?

## Recommendation

BUILD_V0_1 for the Research Adversary / Assumption Extractor / Constraint Analyzer.

Do not build AI Scientist as the primary pillar. Invest long-term architecture effort in Research Director / Meta-Research / Research Allocation.
