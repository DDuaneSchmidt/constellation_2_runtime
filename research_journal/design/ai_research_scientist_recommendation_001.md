# AI Research Scientist Recommendation 001

## Scope

This is design-only recommendation documentation. It does not create production code or operational authority.

## Final Recommendation

BUILD_V0_1.

Scientist-as-primary-pillar is denied.

Scientist-as-supporting-adversarial-service is approved.

The practical V0.1 concept should be named:

Research Adversary / Assumption Extractor / Constraint Analyzer

## Rationale

Atlas benefits more from adversarial critique than from a broad AI Scientist architecture. The immediate bottleneck is not generating more confident narratives. The bottleneck is identifying weak assumptions, rival explanations, fragile regimes, missing constraints, and falsification experiments before candidates consume paper-forward capacity.

## Allowed Outputs

- AnomalyNarrative
- MechanismProposal
- CompetingExplanation
- NullExplanation
- AssumptionExtraction
- ConstraintAnalysis
- ExperimentProposal
- FalsificationProposal
- BeliefUpdateProposal

## Forbidden Outputs

- TradeRecommendation
- CapitalRecommendation
- PositionSizing
- PortfolioAllocation
- CandidatePromotion
- ReplayOverride
- QualificationOverride
- GovernanceOverride

## Required Artifact Questions

- Why might this be true?
- Why might this be false?
- What assumptions are required?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence weakens it?
- What would falsify it?
- What experiment should test it?
- What authority does this artifact NOT have?

## Build Shape

V0.1 should consume research artifacts and return constrained critique artifacts. It should not write directly into qualification, replay, promotion, trading, or capital systems.

## Priority Note

Research Director / Meta-Research / Research Allocation are higher long-term priorities. The V0.1 adversary should support those future systems by improving evidence quality and reducing false conviction.
