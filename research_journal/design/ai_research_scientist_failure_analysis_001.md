# AI Research Scientist Failure Analysis 001

## Scope

This is design-only documentation. It does not create production code, runtime routing, trading authority, or capital authority.

## Core Failure Risk

Scientist-as-primary-pillar is denied because it can create false confidence, narrative overreach, and opaque authority drift. Atlas must not let a generative research service override historical replay, edge qualification, governance, or paper-forward observation evidence.

Scientist-as-supporting-adversarial-service is approved only when its job is to challenge, constrain, and falsify.

## Practical V0.1 Name

Research Adversary / Assumption Extractor / Constraint Analyzer

## Failure Modes

- Narrative confidence exceeds evidence quality.
- Mechanism proposals appear plausible but lack replay support.
- Alternative explanations are not considered.
- Null explanations are omitted.
- Governance language becomes advisory or promotional.
- Outputs are mistaken for qualification decisions.
- Human review treats critique as approval.
- Replay or candidate gates are bypassed by persuasive text.

## Required Artifact Questions

- Why might this be true?
- Why might this be false?
- What assumptions are required?
- What evidence supports it?
- What evidence weakens it?
- What would falsify it?
- What experiment should test it?
- What authority does this artifact NOT have?

## Allowed Outputs

- AnomalyNarrative
- MechanismProposal
- CompetingExplanation
- NullExplanation
- AssumptionExtraction
- ConstraintAnalysis
- ExperimentProposal
- FalsificationProposal
- BeliefUpdateProposal

## Forbidden Outputs

- TradeRecommendation
- CapitalRecommendation
- PositionSizing
- PortfolioAllocation
- CandidatePromotion
- ReplayOverride
- QualificationOverride
- GovernanceOverride

## Failure-Control Recommendation

BUILD_V0_1 with strict artifact boundaries.

The V0.1 service should be evaluated by whether it improves falsification quality, assumption clarity, and experiment design. It should not be evaluated by whether it produces more candidates.

Research Director / Meta-Research / Research Allocation remain higher long-term priorities.
